REJECTED
Reason: The implementation covers all named-constant extractions, all pre-commit and CI wiring, and the `_area_checks()` / `_process_attack_area()` / `_process_character_area()` decomposition correctly. However, the `_handle_timers(delta)` dispatcher deviates from the approved spec. Both `proposal.md` and `tasks.md` explicitly require exactly four delegation calls — `_tick_glide_timer`, `_tick_daze_timer`, `_tick_blood_timer`, `_tick_invincibility_timer` — with `_handle_death_state` and `_handle_flicker` called internally from within `_tick_invincibility_timer`. The actual implementation promotes `_handle_death_state(delta)` and `_handle_flicker()` to direct top-level calls in the `_handle_timers` body (six calls total), and `_tick_invincibility_timer` no longer delegates to either of them. This violates the structural contract stated in the spec and breaks the single-responsibility grouping the refactor was meant to enforce (death state and flicker are invincibility concerns, not top-level timer concerns).

Fix required:
- Move the `_handle_death_state(delta)` call out of `_handle_timers` and into `_tick_invincibility_timer(delta)` as the first statement (guarded by `if health <= 0:`), matching the spec's proposed implementation.
- Move the `_handle_flicker()` call out of `_handle_timers` and into the end of `_tick_invincibility_timer(delta)`, matching the spec's proposed implementation.
- Reduce `_handle_timers(delta)` body to exactly four delegation calls: `_tick_glide_timer(delta)`, `_tick_daze_timer(delta)`, `_tick_blood_timer()`, `_tick_invincibility_timer(delta)`.
